# He Kaiyao — Digital Archive

A buildless static archive with seven complete HTML pages: Home plus /visual-journey, /idea-presentation, /working-log, /project-title, /feedback, and /about.

Serve dist/ with a static host that supports directory index files. Each route has its own index.html and works when opened directly, refreshed, or visited without JavaScript. Asset URLs are relative to the site origin. No build step or dependency installation is needed.

The compact fixed top navigation leads to six independent pages. Home also contains the full current content of every section, with direct page links. Enhanced navigation preserves clean paths and browser history, with native View Transitions when available and a subtle fallback. Reduced-motion settings are respected.

Identity: He Kaiyao / Animation and Interactive Media / Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University / 2026.

The three SVG studies are original illustrative interface specimens. Replace these and the records in app.js with personal work when available. Feedback is saved privately in the visitor's browser and is not sent to He Kaiyao.

Design: Swiss grid, experimental typography, thin technical rules, generous whitespace, a paper-white / black / acid-yellow palette.

Home opens with DIGITAL / MEDIA / ARCHIVE and the supplied collection statement. It contains the The Tower of Doxa ASCII presentation, project filtering, local feedback and a new crop experiment with three aspect ratios, pointer dragging, a keyboard-accessible position slider and reset.

The Inspiration panel follows the tower on Home and Idea Presentation. It preserves the supplied Angel’s Egg paragraph and displays the original poster plus four film stills in a responsive reference grid.

The Flooded Tower panel continues after Inspiration on Home and Idea Presentation. The Tower of Babel appears on the left and The Great Flood on the right with the supplied images, original text and setting summary.
